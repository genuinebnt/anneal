# Third-party material in the system design pages

Most of `content/sd` is written for anneal. Some pages adapt text from openly licensed works; each such page says so in
its `credit` field, which the page shows at the end. The works and their licences:

| Work | Author | Licence | Pages that adapt it |
|---|---|---|---|
| [The System Design Primer](https://github.com/donnemartin/system-design-primer) | Donne Martin | [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) | `concepts/caching.toml` |

Works under "no derivatives" or all-rights-reserved terms (ByteByteGo's system-design-101, Karan Pratap Singh's system
design book, Hello Interview, *Designing Data-Intensive Applications*, *System Design Interview*) are linked from
`sources` and never copied. Facts taken from official documentation and papers are cited in `sources`.
